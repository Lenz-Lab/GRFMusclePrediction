Per subject: ground truth vs. predicted

Each line traces one subject from their baseline ratio to their retention ratio, straight from the real model and real test data. Two lines sloping the same way are telling the same story: model and ground truth agree on which direction the ratio moved. But direction isn't the whole picture — where the endpoints actually land is the magnitude of that shift, and a model can get the slope right while badly under- or over-shooting how far it went. So read each panel in two passes: first the angle (did it move the right way?), then the gap between lines at each end (did it move far enough?).

Only the best-performing model per condition is shown (see legend) — every architecture at once was an unreadable tangle of lines. A red-ringed point marks a subject that was a distinct outlier for its model, not just an ordinary miss. pooled-trained isn't included: no pooled weights are saved to disk yet.
